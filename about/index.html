About Close Encoders

Hi, my name is Josh.

I write code and tinker with computers mostly for fun, then occasionally play games when I'm bored.

While I am organized in a professional setting, my personal projects and learning tend to be very arcane. Imagine a raccoon finding something shiny on a whim, then immediately moving to the next shiny once they are bored.

So I made this site as a hub for recording adventures and hopefully documenting the chaos.

My why is simple: as long as it does not hurt others, I believe that with enough effort, anyone can build or do anything they want. You may have to become obsessed, make trade-offs, and even sell your soul in the process... but truly anything.

If you become skilled enough, no one can stop you from pursuing your goals and helping others pursue theirs.
