About Close Encoders

Hi, I'm Josh.

I write code and tinker with computers because I enjoy understanding how things work. I also occasionally play video games when I'm bored.

I’m a software developer with more years of experience than I want to admit, mostly in Java. These days, I spend the majority of my time writing, experimenting, and learning new things, and I hope my journey helps others do the same.

I want this site to feel like a living document. When I learn something new or find a mistake, updating the site should be only a "git push" away. While I’m organized in a professional setting, my personal projects and research tend to be chaotic if left unmanaged. Imagine a raccoon finding something shiny, then moving on to the next shiny once enough time has passed. So, I made this site as a hub for recording adventures and documenting the chaos in a "structured" manner.

My hope is that readers can learn from my mistakes and experiments. I believe that with enough effort, anyone can build and achieve just about anything they set out to do. You may have to become obsessed or make trade-offs, but difficult things are possible.

Content

My focus is mostly on technology with an emphasis on software, but I don't consider myself limited to a specific niche.

I organize this site into a few types of content:

  • Posts are longer articles on topics or projects I explored in more depth.
  • Notes are more concise, practical references. Useful enough to record, but do not require a full article.
  • Updates document significant changes to the site or one of my projects.
  • Stories are short works of fiction.
  • ...

From there, I break down the content further into sub-categories like "gaming" or "hardware" for easier searching.
